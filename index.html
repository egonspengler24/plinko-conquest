Plinko Conquest

Name your colours, pick a soundtrack, and let the balls decide who owns the board.

Name the squares

Paste a list of names instead

One name per line. They fill the squares in order, left to right, top to bottom.

Blank squares use their colour name (Purple, Forest, Teal…).

Background music

Your own file stays on your computer; nothing is uploaded.

Default track: “Keep It Real” by Nick Petrov. Music from Bensound.com.

Starting speed

Every shot takes just one square, so a full game at 1× can take an hour or more. You can change the speed at any time while playing.

How it plays
  • Every colour starts with its own block of squares and a spinning cannon showing a number, which starts at 1. All the cannons turn together at the same constant rate.
  • Every colour has one ball on the plinko board. When it reaches the bottom it lands on x2 or R, then drops in again.
  • x2 doubles that colour's number (1, 2, 4, 8, 16…).
  • R releases the cannon. It fires that many shots in the direction the barrel is pointing at that moment, then the number resets to 1.
  • Each shot takes exactly one square: the first square it meets that isn't its own colour. So a stack of 8 takes 8 squares.
  • A colour with no squares left is out. The last colour standing wins.
