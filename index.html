How it plays
  • Every colour starts with its own block of squares and a spinning cannon showing a number, which starts at 1.
  • Every colour has one ball on the plinko board. When it reaches the bottom it lands on x2 or R, then drops in again.
  • x2 doubles that colour's number (1, 2, 4, 8, 16…).
  • R releases the cannon. It fires that many shots in the direction the barrel is pointing, then the number resets to 1.
  • A shot flies until it hits a square that isn't its own and paints a blob of squares its colour.
  • A colour with no squares left is out. The last colour standing wins. The longer the game runs, the bigger the blobs get, so it always ends.
